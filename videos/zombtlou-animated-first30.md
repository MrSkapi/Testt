# Five Days — animated 30-second test

[Download MP4](https://github.com/MrSkapi/Testt/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/zombtlou-animated-first30.mp4) · [Editable project](https://github.com/MrSkapi/Testt/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/zombtlou-animated-first30-sources.zip) · [Contact sheet](https://github.com/MrSkapi/Testt/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/zombtlou-animated-first30-contact.jpg)

New 30.000-second animation test, 1920×1080, H.264, 24 FPS, AAC audio. The supplied narration starts at its original 00:00 and runs unaltered through 00:30.

This revision adds a walking entrance and stop, phone lift and taps, a laughing reaction, typed messages and individual reaction pops, game controller motion and two game avatars, laboratory dish pickup, lid removal and fungal growth. Camera pushes and moving pencil hatching accompany the action. The room uses stronger teal, burnt orange, warm wood, green and amber accents.

Captions use rounded bold white capitals with a dark outline. Only the currently spoken word turns yellow. Sparse procedural Foley includes steps, phone buzz, clicks and message pops; there is no music.

The source archive contains the editable Canvas/JavaScript scene and actor rig, word timings, captions, sound cue list, original narration, separate audio stems, browser player, build/render scripts and inspection frames. All 720 frames were rendered at 24 FPS with joint reachability and canvas state checks. The exported MP4 was decoded and sampled for visual review, and four audio checks found zero sample lag against the original narration.

This is a preview of the revised animation treatment. The previous complete chapter remains a separate version.
