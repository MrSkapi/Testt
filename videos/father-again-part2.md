**Format:** 1920×1080, 24fps, 477 seconds (7:57), 160 contiguous shots, 11,448 frames. The supplied recording lasts 475.924898 seconds; the last visual hold adds about one second. Original English narration, unchanged speed and order. No subtitles, replacement voice, added music or SFX.

The returning black/orange hero has no hat. The phone and Mother's laptop preserve their teal/mauve independent reconstructions, with the same crooked glasses and patterned shirts. The delivered body uses the teal Father design. The blue car, family spaces and original illustrated 2D/2.5D visual language carry over from Part 1.

New scenes illustrate the update deadline, different conversation histories, live support, product recommendation settings, a one-day demonstration body, the first delayed hug, three coffees without drinking, roof contact, dropped bolts, garage work, the wrist alert, an old repair video, cancellation, farewell and the next-day drive. The final hug responds without delay. Daylight and evening palettes, grounded shadows, parallax, prop inserts, close reactions and deliberate framing cuts carry the emotional progression without restarting zooms.

Continuity checks include the disabled renewal switch staying off, Mother's goodbye to her own copy being a daylight flashback, the physical body not breathing/eating/drinking, and the final email showing an archived still face rather than a live call.

The MP4 is checked for duration, frame count, audio presence and full-stream decoding. Five separated narration excerpts are compared with the original recording. Reports and the input SHA256 are included in the source archive. Rough local ASR is used only for editorial timing and is never rendered as verified captions.

The archive contains `father-v2/`, drawing/animation code, original narration, the final timeline, visual bible, marker CSV and engine/runtime overrides. Installation and rendering commands are in the included README.
