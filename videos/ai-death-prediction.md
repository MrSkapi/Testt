158 edited shots move from warm everyday rooms and city life into the shelter, smoke-filled escape shaft, rooftops and a daylight cafe. Code-drawn 2D characters and environments use layered depth, lighting, atmospheric particles, rain and reflections. The story follows two people whose separate AI protection plans put them in conflict. They survive by comparing evidence and working together, then keep useful warnings while rejecting automated protection.
