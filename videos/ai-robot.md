The returning black-headed hero wears the established orange hoodie without headwear. Milo is a faceless service machine; human-looking representatives share their owners’ designs. New code-drawn environments include an automated apartment, factory, cafe, night bedroom, colorful city, recognition lobby and an evening courtyard with amber lamps and violet shadows.

The film moves from the comfort of total automation into the discovery that two machines have maintained a friendship without their actual people. Actions include breakfast delivery, chair transport, typing, a doorway wave, getting out of bed, walking, a synchronized cup catch, inspecting original messages, turning off interruption filtering, a supported bed-to-chair transfer, and tentative recovery. The ending preserves the ambiguity of the representative’s voluntary return.
