# Five Days — Chapter 2 / Часть 2

Full animated chapter from TLOUV2.mp3. Duration 9:05, 1920×1080, native 24 FPS, H.264/AAC.

The original narration keeps its complete order, timing and unity gain. Sparse Foley is added; there is no music.

The chapter continues the approved colored drawn style: teal cap, dark orange hoodie, black round hands and thin stick legs; animated pencil hatching and camera movement; white outlined word captions with the currently spoken word in yellow.

195 shots include the call, street crossing, window rescue, ladder descent, gate and lock interactions, return home and the final message. Walking uses low steps and almost straight knees. The render checks fixed limb lengths and reachable hand/foot contacts.

Downloads below: one complete MP4, a self-contained editable source ZIP, and a contact sheet. The project includes the HTML player, drawing/animation code, all timed words and captions, original MP3, mixed soundtrack, fonts, Foley code, and render/review scripts.

Video and audio checks are included in the source ZIP. Original and output audio were compared at eight positions to check that the voice has no timing shift. Native rendered frames and short movement sequences were reviewed after encoding.

Repository .bin files are transport chunks; GitHub Actions verifies the SHA-256 and publishes the original single MP4 without re-encoding.
