# AGAIN — Which One Is Your Father?

A complete illustrated suspense chapter built for `fatherV1.1.mp3`: Evan reconnects with a reconstruction of his late father, restores their car, and discovers that Mother's account has become a different person.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 24fps, 8:09, 172 contiguous shots. Original English narration, unchanged speed and order. No subtitles, added music or SFX. The original recording lasts 488.176327 seconds; the finished visual timeline is 489 seconds.

**Design:** the returning black drawn hero in an orange hoodie, without headwear. Father has crooked pale glasses and a teal patterned shirt. Mother's independent reconstruction wears mauve, as narrated. Warm domestic scenes contrast with blue garage/server scenes, turquoise river memories and a violet showroom.

**Animation and editing:** physical hand/prop actions, an engine repair, turning keys, responding instruments, a lifted baby, moving archive files, permission switches, kitchen activity, laptop closing/pushing, a face-down phone, reaction close-ups and planted-foot walking. Wide, medium, insert, over-the-shoulder and document views carry the story. Fixed framing cuts and a shared sequence camera clock avoid restarting zooms; the hallway walk continues across its cut. Physical screens, contact/cast shadows, parallax and foreground blur keep the presentation drawn 2D with depth.

The MP4 was checked for frame count, duration, audio presence and full-stream decoding. Separated narration excerpts are compared against the source recording; the QC report and source SHA256 are included in the ZIP. Rough ASR in the editable project is an editorial aid, never published as verified subtitles.

The source archive contains `father-v1/`, the narration, final shot timeline, drawing/animation code, scene markers, visual bible and engine/runtime overrides. Installation and rendering commands are in the project README.
