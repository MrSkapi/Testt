# Five Days — 30-second reference style study

A new 30-second animation of the first 30 seconds of TLOUV2.mp3, using the supplied supermarket recording as a visual style reference.

1920×1080, native 24 FPS, H.264/AAC. Long rectangular coats, thin black limbs, pale round faces, a teal cap and orange coat, thick drawn outlines, pencil texture, soft warm/cool lighting, close-ups and moving camera shots. Captions show one black English word at a time with a white glow. The day/time/temperature card follows the reference’s paper-card treatment with original story labels.

The actions include pulling and regripping the shutter chain, noticing movement outside, reading the sister’s text, lifting the phone to the ear, opening the entrance door, the neighbor entering, and picking up the first-aid case. The original first 30 seconds of narration retain their order, timing and unity gain. Sparse procedural Foley is added; no music is used.

No footage, audio, channel logo or watermark from the reference is reused. The animation is drawn from editable code. The source ZIP includes the HTML player, scene code, all word timings, original narration, mixed soundtrack, font and its license, Foley code, render scripts and validation records.

The final MP4 was fully decoded and checked for 720 frames, 1080p, 24 FPS and 30.000 seconds. Eight audio checks showed zero timing shift. Keyframes and encoded motion strips were reviewed, including the shutter chain, phone lift, doorway and first-aid case.
